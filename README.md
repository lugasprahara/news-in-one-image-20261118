One-image summary of Wednesday 18 November 2026: a single 1024×1024 infographic carrying 31 verified stories from six continents — 11 illustrated panels (the Gulf war spreading to Kuwait, Trump landing in Shenzhen, Xi and Putin defying the West at APEC, Voyager 1 reaching one light-day out, COP31's widening fault lines, Cyclone Xavier over the Cook Islands, Australia's settler sanctions, the Odesa strikes, Iran's warning of worse to come, Canada's new arms aid and Tencent's earnings miss) plus a schematic "Where it happened" map and 20 further briefs. The design is an original deep-teal, warm-paper and coral newspaper layout with hand-drawn flat SVG scenes throughout. Every story was checked against reputable reporting dated to that Wednesday — including an explicit day-check per story in SOURCES.md — and widely circulated wrong-day items (the Kerch Bridge collapse, the measles-status loss, the Knesset speaker vote and the Ebola figures, all Tuesday's news) were deliberately left out. The artwork lives in index.html and is rendered to entry.png; no external images or fonts are used.

## Run it

mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
